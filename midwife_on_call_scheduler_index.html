Month & Generation

Role priority is always First Call → Second Call → Clinic → Off. Recurring clinic days are assigned automatically Monday through Friday. Clinic is never scheduled on weekends. On clinic dates, the generator avoids putting the clinic midwife on call unless no other available midwife can cover First or Second Call.

Midwives & Role Targets

Enter preferred monthly role targets or leave them blank for Auto. Select recurring Monday–Friday clinic days for each midwife; those assignments are placed automatically before the rest of the schedule is generated. Clinic is not used on Saturdays or Sundays.
